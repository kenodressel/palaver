**New: voices told apart on the microphone.** A call held over a phone's or the Mac's speaker, or a meeting around the table, is all on the microphone. When nobody spoke on the call's track, Palaver now tells the microphone's voices apart: what sounds like you stays your lines, everyone else becomes a speaker you can name, who is recognized next time like anyone on a call. Your own voice comes from your microphone in your ten latest calls and stays on this Mac (`~/.config/palaver/own-voice.json`).
