**New: corrected lines teach Palaver the voice.** When you say a transcript line was someone else's ("This line is…"), Palaver now learns that person's voice from the corrected lines, so they are recognized in the next meeting even if they were mixed into another speaker this time. It learns in the background when nothing records or transcribes, from at least 4 s of a person's corrected lines; your own lines, the microphone's and rooms teach nothing. Undoing a correction takes back what it taught (#35).
