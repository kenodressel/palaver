**New: the transcript shows where your mic was muted in the call.** While you're muted in Teams or Slack, Palaver doesn't keep your microphone; the transcript now marks that stretch, e.g. "Muted in Teams 16:03–16:05 · your mic wasn't recorded", both live while recording and on the meeting's page afterwards. While you're still muted it says "since 16:03".

**Changed**
- The menu bar shows a muted mic only by the icon's slashed-mic badge; the 🔇 next to the time is gone (it is a muted speaker, not a muted mic).

**Fixed**
- Notes and answers with the Claude Code CLI no longer hang while the Mac is busy, e.g. transcribing: Palaver now hands the CLI its prompt at once instead of waiting for a free system thread.
- A live meeting's menu (More actions, a speaker's actions) no longer closes by itself when new live text arrives near the end of the page.
- The dashed lines around a pause in the transcript show again.
- Security and reliability fixes from a code review: the window's pages are served with a Content-Security-Policy and nosniff, error messages from Notion and the language models no longer repeat what the service sent, keys in the Keychain are updated in place, and a command that hangs while setting up Claude access is stopped reliably.
