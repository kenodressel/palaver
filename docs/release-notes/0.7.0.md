**New: meeting series of your own.** Put a recording into a series from its ⋯ menu (Add to Series…), or type a new name to start one. A series has its own page: rename it, give it a project, see its meetings newest first, merge two series into one. A meeting keeps its series and title even when its calendar event disappears. Search knows `series:"…"`, and Claude can ask for the last meetings of a series (#39, #24).

**New: meetings from calendars that aren't on this Mac.** Without a calendar event, Palaver takes the title and participants from the Teams call window (never the chat or captions), and recognizes a recurring call the next time (#24).

**New: follow-ups after a call.** "Draft Recap Email" opens a draft to the invitees in your mail app. "Add my next steps" sends your next steps to Todoist or to a Notion to-do database (Settings → Follow-ups) (#18).

**New: live assistant.** During a call, the Live view can catch you up ("What did I miss?"), list the next steps so far, or answer a question about the call so far. It only runs when you click, with the language model you chose (#15).

**New: note templates.** 1:1, customer call, interview, workshop, stand-up, or your own; a series remembers its template (#13).

**New: Markdown.** Save a meeting as Markdown from its ⋯ menu, find a readable `meeting.md` in every exported zip, or mirror all meetings into a folder, e.g. an Obsidian vault (Settings → Storage) (#17).

**New: import Notion AI meeting notes** from before Palaver, through Claude (needs an active Claude subscription; see the README) (#4).

**Changed**
- Two-person calls come out as two speakers more often: nearly identical voices are merged, and the number of people invited caps the speakers on the other side (#21).
- Glossary: when you respell a suggestion to a known term, what was heard becomes its alias and the transcripts are corrected right away; the field suggests your glossary's terms (#38). Suggestions no longer offer singular/plural or other forms of the same word as corrections (#36).
- Notion: Settings show where uploads go, each uploaded meeting links its page, and a meeting kept on this Mac is never uploaded (#34).
- **Anonymous usage numbers, on by default.** Once a day, and only while "Look for updates automatically" is on, Palaver sends its version, the macOS version and a random install ID, nothing else. Settings → Updates shows the request exactly as sent, has the public page with everyone's numbers, and turns it off (#6).
